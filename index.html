HOW TO PLAY

You and your friends broke into HappyLand Family Fun Center, closed since 2018. The doors locked behind you. Something inside still thinks the party is going.

W A S D Move
MOUSE Look (click to capture)
SHIFT Sprint (stamina)
E Interact / hide / revive-mash
F Flashlight
C CCTV (at security desk)
TAB Collected lore
ESC Pause

Restore power. Complete the party tasks. The clowns follow sound — running, arcade machines and popped balloons are loud. Balloons drifting toward you mean Bobby is close. When the music stops, Jingle is close. If a friend is standing perfectly still… check twice.

Downed once: mash E to get back up. Downed twice: the party keeps you.

CREDITS

CLOWN HOUSE: AFTER HOURS

A playable concept prototype for a multiplayer Roblox horror game.

HappyLand, PartyKeeper, Bobby, Jingle, Mimic, Tickets and Mr. Happy are original creations for this project. Any resemblance to real indoor playgrounds, living or closed, is nostalgic but coincidental.

Design, code, art direction and procedural audio: this prototype build.
Roblox Studio / Luau implementation files: roblox/ folder.
Full design document: docs/GAME_DESIGN.md.

No clowns were harmed. Several were awakened.
